About Us

Bringing Fresh Produce Closer to You

Black Pearl General Trading FZE LLC is a Dubai-based general trading company focused on the international trade of fresh fruits and vegetables.

We aim to connect growers, suppliers, wholesalers, retailers, and food businesses through dependable sourcing and professional trade coordination. With a focus on product quality, freshness, and customer requirements, we strive to make fresh produce accessible across markets.

Our Vision

To become a trusted name in the global fresh produce trade by connecting markets with quality fruits and vegetables.

Our Mission

To facilitate dependable produce sourcing and international trade through strong partnerships, customer focus, and attention to quality.
